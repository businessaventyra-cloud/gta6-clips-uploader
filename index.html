GTA6 Clips Uploader

GTA6 Clips Uploader is a private, single-operator tool. It builds short "Top 5" gaming compilations from Twitch clips, with on-screen credit to every streamer, and uploads the finished videos to the operator's own YouTube and TikTok accounts.

How it works

  1. The tool reads the most viewed recent clips of one game from the official Twitch API.
  2. It renders a vertical 9:16 countdown video (#5 to #1) on the operator's computer. Each segment shows the clip title and the streamer's channel name; the description lists every source channel.
  3. It uploads the video to one of the operator's own YouTube channels through YouTube API Services (videos.insert) or to the operator's own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API, at most a few videos per account per day.

Who can use it

The tool is not offered to the public. It runs only on the operator's computer and is authorized only for YouTube channels the operator owns. There are no user accounts and no sign-up.
